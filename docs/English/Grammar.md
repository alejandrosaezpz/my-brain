# Grammar

## Orden de la frase y tipos de palabras

| Estructura | Uso | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Subject + verb + object | Acción sobre un objeto. | Governments should reduce plastic waste. |
| Subject + verb + complement | Describir estados. | The trend was significant. |
| Subject + verb + adverbial | Añadir tiempo, lugar, modo o razón. | The figure rose sharply between 2000 and 2010. |
| Subject + verb + object + object | Dar, ofrecer, enseñar o enviar. | Education gives young people better opportunities. |

En inglés casi siempre necesitas sujeto: ~~Is important to reduce pollution.~~ → **It is important to reduce pollution.**

| Tipo de palabra | Función | Ejemplos |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Noun | Persona, cosa o idea. | education, pollution, government |
| Verb | Acción o estado. | increase, decline, believe, become |
| Adjective | Describe un nombre. | important, harmful, gradual |
| Adverb | Modifica un verbo, adjetivo o frase. | significantly, clearly, generally |
| Preposition | Relación de tiempo, lugar o causa. | in, on, at, by, for, with |
| Conjunction | Une ideas. | because, although, whereas, while |

## Be / have / do

| Verbo | Uso | Formas / Estructura | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| be | Ser/estar; pasiva; continuous. | am/is/are/was/were/been/being | The policy is effective. / The data were collected in 2020. |
| have | Tener; auxiliar de perfect tenses. | have/has/had + past participle | Many countries have introduced new laws. |
| do | Auxiliar en preguntas y negativas. | do/does/did + infinitive | This does not mean that taxes are always unfair. |

## Tiempos verbales

### Present and past

| Tiempo | Estructura | Uso | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Present simple | I/you/we/they + verb; he/she/it + verb-s | Hechos generales, rutinas y opiniones. | Education plays a key role in social mobility. |
| Present continuous | am/is/are + verb-ing | Acciones en progreso, cambios actuales o temporales. | More people are choosing to work remotely. |
| Past simple | subject + verb-ed / irregular | **Acciones ya terminadas en un momento pasado concreto:** yesterday, last week/month/year, in 2019, two days ago, when I was... | I visited Rome last summer. No: ~~I have visited Rome last summer.~~ |
| Past continuous | was/were + verb-ing | Acción en progreso en un momento pasado. | The number of users was increasing steadily during this period. |
| Present perfect | have/has + past participle | **Experiencia hasta ahora, resultado presente o periodo no terminado:** ever, never, already, yet, still, since, for, so far, recently, lately, this week/month/year, over the last decade. | I have never been to Africa. / I have travelled to three countries this year. Experiencia + detalle pasado: I have been to Paris three times. I went there last year. |
| Present perfect continuous | have/has been + verb-ing | Acción iniciada antes que continúa o tiene efecto ahora; enfatiza duración/proceso. | Governments have been trying to limit plastic consumption. |
| Past perfect | had + past participle | Acción anterior a otra acción o punto pasado. | After the policy had been implemented, emissions fell. |
| Past perfect continuous | had been + verb-ing | Proceso anterior a un punto pasado. | The population had been growing for years before it stabilised. |

#### Present simple

Describe hechos generales, rutinas y opiniones. Con he/she/it, el verbo afirmativo lleva -s; después de does, vuelve a la forma base.

| Forma | Estructura | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Afirmativa | Subject + verb / verb-s | She works from home. |
| Negativa | Subject + do/does not + verb | She does not work from home. |
| Pregunta | Do/Does + subject + verb? | Does she work from home? |

#### Present continuous

Describe una acción en progreso o una situación temporal en el presente.

| Forma | Estructura | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Afirmativa | Subject + am/is/are + verb-ing | She is working from home today. |
| Negativa | Subject + am/is/are not + verb-ing | She is not working from home today. |
| Pregunta | Am/Is/Are + subject + verb-ing? | Is she working from home today? |

#### Past simple

Describe una acción terminada en un momento pasado concreto. En negativas y preguntas, did lleva la marca de pasado y el verbo vuelve a la forma base.

| Forma | Estructura | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Afirmativa | Subject + verb-ed / irregular past | She visited Rome last summer. |
| Negativa | Subject + did not + verb | She did not visit Rome last summer. |
| Pregunta | Did + subject + verb? | Did she visit Rome last summer? |

#### Past continuous

Describe una acción que estaba en progreso en un momento pasado; puede servir de contexto para otro evento.

| Forma | Estructura | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Afirmativa | Subject + was/were + verb-ing | She was working when I called. |
| Negativa | Subject + was/were not + verb-ing | She was not working when I called. |
| Pregunta | Was/Were + subject + verb-ing? | Was she working when I called? |

#### Present perfect

Conecta el pasado con el presente: experiencia hasta ahora, resultado actual o periodo todavía abierto. No sitúa la acción en un momento pasado terminado.

| Forma | Estructura | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Afirmativa | Subject + have/has + past participle | She has finished the report. |
| Negativa | Subject + have/has not + past participle | She has not finished the report. |
| Pregunta | Have/Has + subject + past participle? | Has she finished the report? |

#### Present perfect continuous

Enfatiza la duración o el proceso de una acción iniciada antes que continúa o tiene un efecto ahora.

| Forma | Estructura | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Afirmativa | Subject + have/has been + verb-ing | She has been studying for two hours. |
| Negativa | Subject + have/has not been + verb-ing | She has not been studying for two hours. |
| Pregunta | Have/Has + subject + been + verb-ing? | Has she been studying for two hours? |

#### Past perfect

Describe una acción que ya había ocurrido antes de otra acción o punto de referencia pasado.

| Forma | Estructura | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Afirmativa | Subject + had + past participle | She had left before I arrived. |
| Negativa | Subject + had not + past participle | She had not left before I arrived. |
| Pregunta | Had + subject + past participle? | Had she left before I arrived? |

#### Past perfect continuous

Enfatiza la duración de una actividad que estaba en progreso antes de otro momento pasado.

| Forma | Estructura | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Afirmativa | Subject + had been + verb-ing | She had been working for two hours when I arrived. |
| Negativa | Subject + had not been + verb-ing | She had not been working for two hours when I arrived. |
| Pregunta | Had + subject + been + verb-ing? | Had she been working for two hours when I arrived? |

### Future forms

| Forma | Uso | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| will + infinitive | Predicción, decisión o consecuencia. | This will probably increase inequality. |
| be going to + infinitive | Plan o predicción con evidencia. | The number is going to rise if no action is taken. |
| Present continuous | Plan organizado. | I am taking an English exam next month. |
| Present simple | Horarios oficiales. | The course starts in September. |
| Future continuous: will be + verb-ing | Acción en progreso en un momento futuro. | More people will be working from home in the future. |
| Future perfect: will have + past participle | Algo terminado antes de un momento futuro. | By 2030, many companies will have adopted AI tools. |

### For / since / how long / when

| Elemento | Uso | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| for + period of time | Duración total. | I have studied English for three years. |
| since + starting point | Punto de inicio. | I have studied English since 2020. |
| How long + have/has + subject + past participle? | Preguntar por duración hasta ahora. | How long have you lived in Madrid? |
| When + past simple? | Preguntar por un momento pasado concreto. | When did you move to Madrid? |

Si la situación continúa hasta ahora, usa present perfect con for/since/how long. No: ~~for 2020~~, ~~since three years~~, ~~When have you moved?~~

## Modal verbs y probabilidad

| Forma | Uso | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| can | Capacidad, posibilidad general o permiso informal. | People can access information instantly. |
| could | Habilidad pasada, posibilidad o cortesía. | This could lead to better public health. |
| may | Posibilidad o permiso formal. | The results may vary depending on the country. |
| might | Posibilidad incierta o hipotética. | This might explain why the figures changed. |
| must | Obligación fuerte o deducción. | Schools must protect students' privacy. |
| have to | Obligación externa. | Citizens have to follow environmental regulations. |
| mustn't | Prohibición. | Companies mustn't misuse personal data. |
| don't have to | Falta de necesidad. | Students do not have to study abroad to become successful. |
| should / ought to / need to | Consejo, recomendación o necesidad; should también expresa expectativa. | Schools should teach digital literacy. |
| would | Hipótesis, cortesía, consecuencia condicional o hábitos pasados. | This would make public transport more attractive. |
| can't / cannot | Deducción negativa fuerte. | This cannot be explained by economic factors alone. |
| is likely/unlikely to | Probabilidad. | The policy is likely to benefit low-income families. |
| will probably / may well | Resultado probable. | Remote work may well become more common in the future. |

### Modal + have + past participle

| Estructura | Uso | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| must have + past participle | Deducción fuerte sobre el pasado. | The campaign must have influenced public opinion. |
| may/might/could have + past participle | Posibilidad pasada. | The rise in prices may have discouraged consumers. |
| can't/couldn't have + past participle | Imposibilidad pasada. | The fall could not have been caused by one factor alone. |
| should have + past participle | Crítica o acción que debería haber ocurrido. | Authorities should have invested earlier. |

## Questions y question tags

| Tipo | Estructura | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Yes/no | Auxiliary + subject + verb? | Do people spend too much time online? |
| Wh-question | Wh-word + auxiliary + subject + verb? | Why do people prefer private cars? |
| Subject question | Who/What + verb? | What causes this problem? |
| Indirect question | Could you explain + wh-word + subject + verb? | Could you explain why this trend has changed? |

Las preguntas indirectas **no llevan inversión**: ~~I do not know why is it important.~~ → **I do not know why it is important.**

| Question tag | Regla | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frase positiva | Tag negativa. | It is expensive, isn't it? |
| Frase negativa | Tag positiva. | People do not always agree, do they? |
| Sin auxiliar | Usar do/does/did. | She works abroad, doesn't she? |
| Imperativo | ..., will you? | Explain your opinion clearly, will you? |
| Let's | ..., shall we? | Let's move on to the next point, shall we? |

## Negatives

| Forma | Uso | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| not | Negación básica. | This is not always true. |
| no + noun | Negación más directa. | There is no simple solution. |
| never | Negación fuerte. | This should never be ignored. |
| hardly / scarcely / rarely | Cantidad o frecuencia casi nula. | This is rarely discussed. |
| no longer / any longer | Algo que ya no ocurre. | University education is no longer affordable for many families. |

## There is / it is y referencias

| Estructura | Uso | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| There is/are... | Existencia. | There are several reasons for this trend. |
| It is + adjective + to infinitive | Valoración general; sujeto anticipado. | It is essential to protect the environment. |
| It is clear that... | Introducir una idea. | It is clear that education influences employment. |
| it | Referirse a algo mencionado. | Technology is useful, but it can be expensive. |
| this | Referirse a una idea anterior cercana. | Many children spend hours online. This can affect their concentration. |
| that | Referirse a una idea anterior o contrastarla con otra. | Some people prefer private transport, but that is not always sustainable. |
| such + noun | Evitar repetir una referencia. | Such measures could reduce pollution. |
| such a/an + adjective + singular noun | Enfatizar una cosa singular. | This is such a complex issue that simple solutions rarely work. |
| do so | Sustituir una acción mencionada. | Governments should invest in public transport, and they should do so as soon as possible. |

## Articles y sustantivos

| Artículo | Uso | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| a/an | Primera mención, uno cualquiera o profesión. | A tax could reduce plastic use. |
| the | Algo específico, único o ya mencionado. | The government should invest more in public transport. |
| Zero article | Plurales o incontables en sentido general. | People need access to education. |

En sentido general: **society**, no ~~the society~~; **pollution**, no ~~the pollution~~.

| Tipo de sustantivo | Regla | Ejemplos |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Countable | Tiene singular y plural. | an idea / many ideas |
| Uncountable | No lleva a/an ni plural normal. | information, advice, research, evidence, pollution, traffic |

**Formas correctas:** some information, a piece of advice, much research, strong evidence.

### Quantifiers

| Forma | Se usa con | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| many / few / several | Contables plurales. | Many students study abroad. |
| much / little | Incontables. | There is little evidence to support this view. |
| a lot of / plenty of | Ambos tipos. | A lot of money is spent on advertising. |
| a number of | Contables plurales; verbo plural. | A number of countries have banned plastic bags. |
| the number of | Verbo singular. | The number of cars has increased. |

| Tipo | Posición | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Adjective | Antes del nombre o después de be/seem/become. | a significant increase / The increase was significant. |
| Adverb | Modifica verbo, adjetivo o frase. | The figure increased significantly. |

## Comparatives y superlatives

| Forma | Comparativo | Superlativo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Adjetivos cortos | taller than / cheaper than | the tallest / the cheapest |
| Adjetivos largos | more expensive than | the most expensive |
| Irregulares | better / worse / farther or further | the best / the worst / the farthest or furthest |

| Estructura | Uso | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| as + adjective/adverb + as | Igualdad. | Cycling is not as convenient as driving. |
| twice/three times as much as | Proporciones. | The UK spent twice as much as Spain. |
| as much/many as | Enfatizar una cantidad. | The figure reached as much as 80%. |
| get / become / grow + comparative | Cambio gradual; become es más formal. | The streets are getting busier. / The issue is becoming more serious. / The problem is growing worse. |
| The + comparative..., the + comparative... | Dos cosas cambian juntas. | The more I practise, the less nervous I feel. |
| rather than + verb-ing / noun | Contraponer alternativas. | People should use public transport rather than driving alone. |

### Grado de diferencia

| Diferencia | Modificadores | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pequeña | slightly, a bit, a little | This phone is slightly more expensive than that one. |
| Importante | considerably, significantly, noticeably | This course is significantly harder than the beginner level. |
| Grande | far, much, a lot, substantially | This computer is far faster than my old one. |

### Superlativos complejos

| Estructura | Uso | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| one of the + superlative + plural noun | Uno dentro de un grupo destacado. | Paris is one of the most beautiful cities I have visited. |
| among the + superlative + plural noun | Dentro del grupo más destacado; más formal. | That concert was among the best performances I have ever seen. |
| the second/third + superlative | Posición exacta. | Madrid is the second largest city in the European Union. |
| by far / easily the + superlative | Destacar con claridad o mucha diferencia. | This is by far the most effective solution. / It is easily the most convenient option. |

No uses doble comparativo: ~~more better~~ → **better**. Después de *one of the most*, usa plural: **one of the most important factors**.

| Patrón | Después de / Uso | Ejemplo |

| verb + -ing | enjoy, avoid, consider, suggest, recommend, mind, finish, deny, admit, risk | Many people avoid using public transport at night. |
| verb + to + infinitive | want, need, decide, hope, plan, aim, agree, refuse, manage, afford, tend, fail | Governments aim to reduce carbon emissions. |

### Verbos que cambian de significado

| Verbo | + -ing | + to + infinitive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| remember | Recordar algo que hiciste: *I remember locking the door.* | Acordarse de hacer algo: *Remember to lock the door.* |
| forget | Olvidar una experiencia pasada: *I will never forget meeting her.* | Olvidarse de hacer algo: *I forgot to call him.* |
| stop | Dejar una actividad: *He stopped smoking.* | Parar para hacer otra cosa: *He stopped to smoke.* |
| try | Probar un método: *Try drinking more water.* | Intentar hacer algo: *I tried to open the door.* |
| regret | Arrepentirse de algo hecho: *She regrets leaving her job.* | Lamentar informar o decir algo: *We regret to inform you that the application was rejected.* |
| go on | Continuar lo mismo: *He went on talking for an hour.* | Pasar a otra acción: *She went on to discuss solutions.* |
| mean | Implicar: *Studying abroad means spending more money.* | Tener intención: *I meant to send the email yesterday.* |
| need | Necesitar ser hecho, sentido pasivo: *The report needs checking.* | Necesitar hacer algo: *Students need to practise regularly.* |

### Perfect gerund y perfect infinitive

| Forma | Estructura | Uso | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Perfect gerund | having + past participle | Acción anterior a otra. | She admitted having made a mistake. |
| Perfect infinitive | to have + past participle | Infinitivo referido a una acción pasada. | The policy appears to have reduced unemployment. |

**Patrones:** regret/deny/admit having done; claim/appear to have done.

## Have / get something done

No haces tú la acción: organizas o pagas para que otra persona la haga.

| Estructura | Uso / Registro | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| have + object + past participle | Servicios y trámites; más formal que get. | I had my suit tailored for the interview. |
| get + object + past participle | Servicios cotidianos y reparaciones; más informal. | I got my phone screen fixed. |
| have to have + object + past participle | Obligación de encargar algo. | I have to have these contracts reviewed by a lawyer. |
| need to get + object + past participle | Necesidad de encargar algo. | I need to get my laptop repaired before classes start. |
| be getting + object + past participle | Servicio futuro organizado. | We are getting our apartment cleaned next weekend. |

| Contexto | Ejemplos |
| --- | --- |
| Documentos | have my diploma certified; have my passport renewed; have my work permit processed; have my documents translated |
| Servicios personales | get my hair cut; get my eyes checked; get my teeth cleaned; get my photo taken |
| Tecnología | get my phone fixed; get my laptop repaired; have the software installed |
| Casa | get the house painted; have the roof repaired; get the washing machine fixed |

**I repaired my car** = lo reparé yo. **I had my car repaired** = alguien me lo reparó.

## Relative clauses

| Tipo / Pronombre | Uso | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Defining: who/that, which/that | Información esencial; sin comas. | The book that you recommended was excellent. |
| Non-defining: who, which, whose | Información extra; con comas. No usar that ni omitir el pronombre. | The Eiffel Tower, which was built in 1889, attracts millions of visitors. |
| whose | Posesión: personas, organizaciones o cosas. | Students whose parents have low incomes need support. |
| where | Lugar. | This is the city where I learned English. |
| when | Tiempo o periodo. | There was a period when people relied mainly on newspapers. |

### Omitir o reducir

| Caso | Forma completa | Forma reducida |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Defining con pronombre objeto: se puede omitir. | The film that I watched was fascinating. | The film I watched was fascinating. |
| Pronombre sujeto: no se puede omitir. | The man who lives next door is a doctor. | No: ~~The man lives next door is a doctor.~~ |
| -ing: sentido activo. | People who live in cities often use public transport. | People living in cities often use public transport. |
| Past participle: sentido pasivo. | The data which were collected during the experiment were useful. | The data collected during the experiment were useful. |

### Preposiciones y cuantificadores

| Patrón | Ejemplo formal | Alternativa cotidiana |
| --- | --- | --- |
| preposition + which/whom | This is the restaurant in which we had dinner. | This is the restaurant which we had dinner in. |
| with whom | The person with whom I travelled was my cousin. | The person who I travelled with was my cousin. |
| for which | The company for which I work is international. | The company which I work for is international. |

| Estructura | Significado | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| all of which / whom | Todos. | The policy affected several sectors, all of which required support. |
| most of which / whom | La mayoría. | She has ten friends, most of whom live abroad. |
| many of which / whom | Muchos. | The city introduced several measures, many of which were successful. |
| some of which / whom | Algunos. | I bought several books, some of which were about history. |
| none of which / whom | Ninguno. | They recommended several films, none of which I had seen. |
| two/three/four of which / whom | Número concreto. | The company opened five branches, two of which are in Spain. |

### Infinitivos y comentarios con which

| Estructura | Uso | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| the first/second/third/last/only + noun + to + verb | Orden o posición. | She was the first person to answer the question. |
| noun + to + verb | Algo que puede o debe hacerse. | There are several deadlines to meet before the end of the month. |
| statement, which + comment | Comentar toda la frase anterior. | He did not study for the test, which was surprising. |
| statement, which means that... | Explicar consecuencia. | Many people work remotely, which means that office demand may decline. |
| statement, which is why... | Conectar razón y resultado. | Public transport is unreliable, which is why many people drive. |

No sustituyas which por what: ~~He was late, what was annoying.~~ → **He was late, which was annoying.**

## Conditionals

| Tipo | Estructura | Uso | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Zero | If + present, present | Verdades generales. | If prices rise, demand often falls. |
| First | If + present, will/can/may + infinitive | Posibilidad futura real. | If governments invest more, public transport will improve. |
| Second | If + past, would/could/might + infinitive | Hipótesis presente o futura. | If cities were safer, more people would cycle. |
| Third | If + past perfect, would/could/might have + past participle | Hipótesis pasada. | If authorities had acted earlier, the problem might have been avoided. |
| Mixed | If + past perfect, would + infinitive | Pasado con resultado presente. | If cities had invested earlier, traffic would be less severe today. |

### Otras formas de expresar condiciones

| Forma | Significado | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| provided/providing that | Siempre que. | This policy could work, provided that it is properly funded. |
| as long as | Siempre que. | Children can benefit from technology as long as screen time is limited. |
| unless | A menos que. | Pollution will continue to rise unless stricter laws are introduced. |
| otherwise | Si no. | Governments should act now; otherwise, the problem may worsen. |
| in case / in the event that | Por si acaso / en caso de que. | Schools should provide guidance in case students face online risks. |
| supposing/suppose | Imagina que. | Supposing everyone worked from home, city centres would change dramatically. |

Las inversiones con **had, should y were** se recogen en [Inversion](#inversion).

## Wish / if only

| Estructura | Uso | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| wish + past simple | Deseo sobre el presente. | I wish public transport were more reliable. |
| wish + past perfect | Lamento sobre el pasado. | Many people wish they had learned English earlier. |
| wish + would | Deseo de cambio externo. | I wish governments would act more quickly. |

## Reported speech

### Cambios de tiempo

| Direct speech | Reported speech | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Present simple | Past simple | “I live here” → She said she lived there. |
| Present continuous | Past continuous | “I am working” → He said he was working. |
| Past simple | Past perfect | “I went there” → She said she had gone there. |
| Present perfect | Past perfect | “I have finished” → He said he had finished. |
| will | would | “It will increase” → Experts said it would increase. |
| can | could | “It can help” → She said it could help. |
| may | might | “It may change” → They said it might change. |
| must | had to | “We must act” → He said they had to act. |

No siempre hay backshift si la información sigue siendo verdadera: **She said that Madrid is the capital of Spain.**

### Preguntas, órdenes y peticiones

| Tipo | Estructura | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Yes/no question | asked if/whether + subject + verb | “Do you agree?” → She asked whether I agreed. |
| Wh-question | asked + question word + subject + verb | “Why did it fall?” → He asked why it had fallen. |
| Imperative | told + object + to + verb | “Close the door” → She told me to close the door. |
| Negative imperative | told + object + not to + verb | “Don't be late” → He told me not to be late. |
| Polite request | asked + object + to + verb | “Please help me” → She asked me to help her. |

### Tiempo y lugar

| Direct | Reported |
| --- | --- |
| now | then / at that time |
| today | that day |
| yesterday | the day before / the previous day |
| tomorrow | the next day / the following day |
| this / these | that / those |
| here | there |

## Conectores y subordinadas

### Añadir, ejemplificar y concluir

| Función | Expresiones | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Añadir | and, also, moreover, furthermore, in addition | Moreover, this policy could create jobs. |
| Ejemplificar | for example, for instance, a clear example of this is... | For instance, many cities now restrict cars. |
| Concluir | in conclusion, overall, to conclude, on balance | On balance, the benefits outweigh the drawbacks. |

### Concesión y contraste

| Estructura | Uso | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| although / even though + clause | Aunque; even though añade énfasis. | Although taxes can be unpopular, they can change behaviour. |
| despite / in spite of + noun/pronoun/-ing | A pesar de. | Despite being costly, the policy may be effective. |
| despite the fact that + clause | A pesar de que. | Despite the fact that it is expensive, it remains popular. |
| whereas / while + clause | Contrastar dos ideas. | Cars are convenient, whereas buses are more sustainable. |
| but / however / nevertheless / nonetheless | Contraste; los tres últimos pueden introducir una frase nueva. | The system is expensive. Nevertheless, it is necessary. |
| no matter how + adjective | Por muy... | No matter how useful technology is, it cannot replace teachers completely. |

- No combines although + but: ~~Although it is expensive, but it is useful.~~ → **Although it is expensive, it is useful.**
- ~~Despite it was expensive~~ → **Although it was expensive** / **Despite being expensive** / **Despite the fact that it was expensive**.

### Causa y resultado

| Estructura | Uso | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| because / since / as + clause | Causa. | Since housing is expensive, many young people live with their parents. |
| because of / due to / owing to + noun phrase | Causa. | Due to rising prices, families are spending less. |
| lead to / result in / contribute to + noun/-ing | Resultado o contribución. | Poor planning can lead to serious traffic problems. |
| so / as a result / consequently / therefore | Consecuencia. | The cost increased; therefore, demand declined. |
| so/such ... that | Intensidad con consecuencia. | The problem is so serious that immediate action is needed. |

### Tiempo y propósito

| Estructura | Uso | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| when / while / after / before / once + clause | Relación temporal. | Once the law is introduced, behaviour may change. |
| to + infinitive | Propósito. | Governments should invest to improve public services. |
| in order to / so as to + infinitive | Propósito más formal. | New laws are needed in order to protect consumers. |
| so that / in order that + clause | Propósito; permite un sujeto diferente. | Schools should teach finance so that students can manage money. |
| for + noun/-ing | Función. | This money could be used for improving hospitals. |

## Énfasis y cleft sentences

| Estructura | Uso | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| do/does/did + infinitive | Enfatizar una afirmación. | This policy does have some disadvantages. |
| It is undeniable that... | Afirmación fuerte. | It is undeniable that technology has changed education. |
| It is/was + focus + that/who... | Destacar una parte de la frase. | It is education that gives people better opportunities. |
| What + clause + is/was... | Destacar una idea. | What governments need is a long-term strategy. |
| The (main) reason why... is that... | Destacar una causa. | The reason why many people drive is that buses are unreliable. |

## Inversion

Se usa para énfasis y estilo formal.

| Estructura | Uso | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Not only + auxiliary + subject + verb, but... | Añadir con énfasis. | Not only does pollution harm health, but it also damages the economy. |
| Rarely/Seldom + auxiliary + subject + verb | Frecuencia negativa. | Rarely do governments solve such problems quickly. |
| Only when + clause + auxiliary + subject + verb | Condición enfática. | Only when prices rise will consumers change their habits. |
| Had + subject + past participle | Third conditional formal. | Had the policy been introduced earlier, the results might have been better. |
| Should + subject + verb | First conditional formal. | Should there be a problem, contact the manager. |
| Were + subject + to + verb | Second conditional formal. | Were governments to invest more, the situation would improve. |

## Nominalisation

Convertir verbos o adjetivos en nombres para un estilo más formal.

| Forma verbal / adjetival | Forma nominal |
| --- | --- |
| People consume more plastic. | There has been an increase in plastic consumption. |
| The economy improved. | There was an improvement in the economy. |
| The government decided to ban it. | The government's decision to ban it was controversial. |
| Cities are becoming more crowded. | Urban overcrowding is becoming a serious issue. |

## Hedging

| Expresión | Uso | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| tend to | Generalización cuidadosa. | Young people tend to rely on social media. |
| appear/seem to | Observación prudente. | The policy appears to be effective. |
| to some extent | Matizar. | I agree with this view to some extent. |
| it is likely/unlikely that | Probabilidad. | It is likely that demand will decrease. |
| arguably | Opinión defendible. | This is arguably the most effective solution. |

Para posibilidades con may/might/could, consulta [Modal verbs y probabilidad](#modal-verbs-y-probabilidad).

## Errores frecuentes

| Incorrecto | Correcto | Motivo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| People is... | People are... | People es plural. |
| I am agree. | I agree. | Agree es un verbo, no un adjetivo. |
| It depends of... | It depends on... | Preposición correcta. |
| In the other hand | On the other hand | Expresión fija. |
| I have 23 years. | I am 23 years old. | La edad se expresa con be. |
