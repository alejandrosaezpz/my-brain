# Writing

## Overview

Develop IELTS Writing plans, sample responses, and revision notes for both tasks here.

## Task 1

Collect notes on describing charts, processes, maps, or letters for the test format you are preparing for.

## Task 2

Record essay plans, thesis statements, examples, and revisions for common prompt types.

## Revision checklist

- answer every part of the prompt
- organize ideas into clear paragraphs and support them with examples
- check grammar, vocabulary, spelling, and word count
