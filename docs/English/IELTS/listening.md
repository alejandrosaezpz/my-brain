# IELTS Listening

## Exam at a Glance

- 40 questions across 4 parts, with difficulty increasing as the test progresses.
- The audio is played once. Answers generally follow the order of the recording.
- Common question types: multiple choice, matching, form/note/sentence completion, maps and plans, and short-answer questions.

## Core Strategy

**READ → PREDICT → LISTEN FOR PARAPHRASES → WRITE → MOVE ON**

- Read the questions before the audio starts and identify the key words.
- Predict the answer type: noun, number, place, adjective, and so on.
- Expect paraphrases rather than the exact wording in the question.
- Keep following the audio if you miss an answer. Never sacrifice several questions trying to recover one.

## Before Listening

- Check the word limit.
- Predict the answer types.
- Identify how multiple-choice options differ.
- Locate starting points and landmarks on maps.
- Mentally highlight important keywords.

## Common IELTS Traps

### Distractors

Speakers may mention one answer, then correct or change it.

> We originally planned to meet on Tuesday, but we've moved it to Thursday.

Correct answer: **Thursday**

Watch for: `but`, `however`, `actually`, `instead`, `originally`, `eventually`, `in fact`, `although`.

### Paraphrasing

The question and recording often express the same idea with different vocabulary. For example: `problem` → `difficulty`.

### Singular and Plural

Listen for the ending: missing an `-s` can make an answer incorrect.

### Spelling

Spelling matters, especially for names, places and longer words.

### Numbers

Listen carefully for:

- 13 vs 30, 14 vs 40, and 15 vs 50
- Dates, prices, phone numbers, percentages, times and measurements

### Word Limits

Follow instructions exactly. **NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER** means your answer must stay within that limit.

## If I Miss an Answer

> **GUESS → MOVE ON → KEEP LISTENING**

Losing one question is better than losing several because you stopped following the recording.

## Multiple Choice

- Compare the options before listening.
- Focus on what makes A, B and C different.
- Listen for meaning, not exact words.
- Be careful: an option may be mentioned and later rejected.

## Maps and Plans

Useful vocabulary: `opposite`, `next to`, `beside`, `between`, `across from`, `beyond`, `at the end of`, `in the corner`, `on the left/right`, `go past`, `turn left/right`.

1. Find the starting point.
2. Identify fixed landmarks.
3. Follow the speaker's route continuously.
4. Do not mentally restart if you miss one location.

## Form / Note / Sentence Completion

- Predict the grammatical category of the missing information.
- Check the surrounding grammar.
- Listen carefully for numbers and proper nouns.
- Respect the word limit and check singular/plural.

## Error Analysis

After each practice, classify mistakes:

- [ ] I did not understand the audio.
- [ ] I missed a paraphrase.
- [ ] I fell for a distractor.
- [ ] I lost concentration.
- [ ] I was still thinking about the previous question.
- [ ] Spelling mistake.
- [ ] Singular/plural mistake.
- [ ] Number/date/measurement mistake.
- [ ] Word-limit mistake.
- [ ] I could not read the options quickly enough.

## Golden Rules

- Read ahead and predict the answer.
- Listen for meaning and changes of mind.
- Follow the word limit; check spelling and singular/plural.
- If you miss one, guess and keep going.
