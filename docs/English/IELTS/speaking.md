# Speaking

## Overview

Prepare ideas and review IELTS Speaking practice across all three parts here.

## Parts

- **Part 1:** short questions about familiar topics
- **Part 2:** a longer response based on a cue card
- **Part 3:** discussion of broader and more abstract questions

## Practice routine

- record a response and note its duration
- review clarity, organization, grammar, and range of vocabulary
- identify one specific improvement for the next attempt
